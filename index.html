    Name

    Tidewhistle Isle

    Four islanders live here with their own personalities, needs, friendships and grudges. You are the Sky Hand. Watch over them, or meddle.

    Controls

    • Click an islander to see what they're thinking.
    • Press and hold an islander to pick them up. Drag them around. Flick to throw.
    • Drag empty ground, or use WASD / arrows, to move the camera. Scroll to zoom.
    • Hover a building, or press R, to look inside.
    • Space pause · 1–5 speed · Tab next islander · F follow · N names · L log
